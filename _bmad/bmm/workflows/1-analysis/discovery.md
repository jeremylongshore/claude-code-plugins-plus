# Discovery Workflow

## Phase
1 - Analysis

## Agent
Analyst

## Purpose
Conduct initial business discovery to understand the problem space, stakeholders, and high-level requirements.

## Steps

1. **Context Gathering**
   - What business problem are we solving?
   - Who are the target users?
   - What are the business goals?

2. **Stakeholder Mapping**
   - Identify all stakeholders
   - Understand their needs and priorities
   - Map influence and interest levels

3. **Constraint Identification**
   - Budget and timeline constraints
   - Technical constraints
   - Regulatory requirements

4. **Documentation**
   - Create analysis document
   - Save to `_bmad-output/analysis.md`

## Output
- `_bmad-output/analysis.md`

## Next
Proceed to Requirements Gathering or directly to Phase 2 (Create PRD)
