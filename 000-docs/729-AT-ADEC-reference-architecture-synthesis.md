## 0. DECISION — one direction, not a menu

**Adopt a three-chain, tier-gated reference architecture: an authoring chain that humans write, a projection chain that only machines write, and an evidence chain that runs on its own cadence and touches the other two by reference only. Create no new authority. Generate every projection. Gate every generated surface. Publish the tier honestly. Never let a mirror, an adapter, a package, or a website page speak for the skill.**

Concretely, that resolves to:

1. **Two-layer identity** — a small, rigidly uniform `SKILL.md` frontmatter (the existing IS 8 plus the already-specified optional families, unchanged) **plus** a closed-schema machine sidecar (`skill-card.yaml`) that carries capabilities, constraints, side effects, upstream pins, adapters, lifecycle, and the evaluation pointer. **The frontmatter is not the machine contract and is not extended.**
2. **Generated, drift-gated harness adapters** — adapters are emitted by generators in `scripts/`, never hand-authored, never byte-identical to canonical content, and never carrying a body, a license, an eval, or a second version.
3. **Mirrors as a distinct ingested class** — quarantined, provenance-bound, never promotable to canonical, carrying attribution through every projection, and never published under the Intent Solutions identity without recorded per-channel consent.
4. **Evidence as a separate chain with classes E0–E3**, retention as a validity condition, no self-approval, and the Dolt ledger as the public append-only record that every benchmarked repository lacks.
5. **Computed tiers T0–T4** rather than a uniform anatomy, because a uniform bar at 3,179 skills either sits at the floor or leaves ~90% permanently non-compliant.
6. **No renames.** `plugins/<category>/<plugin>/skills/<name>/`, the top-level `skills/` curriculum + `.curated` mirror, and the public install slug `jeremylongshore/claude-code-plugins` are all frozen compatibility contracts.

**Recommended execution:** land the provenance/licensing quarantine first (blueprint § 15's first bead), then the authority freeze and the compliance ratchet, then the canonical contract, and do not begin corpus migration until the ratchet is live and an independent review closes clean.

## 1. CONTEXT — the measured facts that forced this decision

Twelve public agent-skill repositories were reviewed at pinned commits (§ 2). The whole sample contains roughly **741 `SKILL.md`**; the Intent Solutions corpus is **3,179 plugin `SKILL.md`** (re-verified at `HEAD 49210ecb6`). The largest repository in the sample is 10.6% of our size **and is visibly failing at that size**.

The decisive observation is not any single good pattern. It is that **every vendor examined has exactly one of the three things a marketplace at our scale needs, and none has all three:**

- NVIDIA has the governance machinery and **no metadata contract** (30 ad-hoc `metadata:` sub-keys; `allowed-tools` on 24% of skills).
- NVIDIA-Medtech has a superb machine contract that **costs too much per skill to reach 12**, let alone 3,179 — and its real gates deliberately run on a maintainer's workstation, not in CI.
- AWS has the best evidence discipline in the sample and **hand-forks its adapters into silent degradation** — its published F1 = 0.960 attaches to a code path most users never take.
- Anthropic and the standards body have the cleanest specification and **zero CI**.

Intent Solutions already owns the three assets that let all three coexist: a **kernel** that can hold the schema so this repo never becomes its own spec authority; **Dolt**, which can hold evidence _and_ serve as the public append-only record every one of these repos lacks; and **tier-gating**, which is the only mechanism that makes a real bar compatible with a real corpus.

The design's thesis is therefore **subtractive**: the repo's three named historical failures — mirror mis-attribution, one-fact-two-claimants (twice), and ~200 silently gitignored docs — are all **authority-multiplication** failures, not capability gaps. The highest-value architectural act available is refusing to create new authorities.

## 3. LICENSING IMPLICATIONS — read before adopting anything

**This is the binding constraint on how the rest of this ADR may be executed.**

### 3.1 The two unlicensed sources

**`anthropics/skills` and `openai/skills` declare NO LICENSE at the repository root.** Under the Berne Convention and US copyright law, absence of a license grant means **all rights reserved** — the default is _no permission_, not _permissive_. Publishing source publicly is not a license.

- `anthropics/skills` carries `license: null` at the repository level. Two of its 17 skills declare no license at all, and one of those has no `LICENSE.txt` either — an unlicensed file inside an unlicensed repository.
- `anthropics/skills` additionally contains four document skills under a **bespoke source-available license that expressly forbids extraction, copying, derivative works, and distribution**. Those skills **cannot be mirrored under any circumstances**, and any mirroring pipeline must treat their license text as a hard refusal, not a warning.
- `openai/skills` has no root LICENSE but ships **44/44 per-skill `LICENSE.txt`**, and they genuinely differ (Apache / MIT+vendor terms / a bespoke non-open license restricting automated and AI-agent use). The per-skill file — not the repo — is the redistribution unit there.

**Therefore:**

> **Architectural PATTERNS are not copyrightable and may be adopted. FILES, schema text, README prose, code, and any other expressive content from those two repositories may NOT be copied or adapted.**

Every "ADOPT" in this ADR whose source is `anthropics/skills` or `openai/skills` is an **independent reimplementation of an idea**, expressed in our own words and our own code, and must remain so. Specifically:

| Idea sourced from an unlicensed repo                                                                 | How it must be executed here                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Per-skill `LICENSE` file as the redistribution unit (openai)                                         | We adopt the _shape_ — a license file scoped to the redistributable unit — and write our own policy text. **No file, filename convention text, or README prose is copied.** |
| A closed frontmatter allowlist where an unknown key is an error (openai, anthropic `quick_validate`) | We already have our own validator with its own `SKILL_FIELDS`. The ratchet is implemented in our code. **No validator code is copied.**                                     |
| An explicit anti-file list inside a skill directory (openai)                                         | Reimplemented as our own authoring rule; **wording is ours**.                                                                                                               |
| A per-vendor sidecar slot (openai `agents/<vendor>.yaml`)                                            | Our equivalent is the generated adapter output described in blueprint § 6. **The idea, not the file.**                                                                      |
| Paired-baseline eval design and held-out description optimization (anthropic harness)                | Reimplemented inside our own `eval-spec.yaml` contract and j-rig. **No harness code, no prompt text, no eval asset is copied.**                                             |
| A real `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` with pinned versions + source URLs (anthropic)                          | We write our own notices file from our own dependency data. **The structure, not the content.**                                                                             |
| The "Principle of Lack of Surprise" framing (anthropic)                                              | Not adopted as text. Our equivalent safety-authoring guidance is written independently.                                                                                     |

**The two repositories may still be cited, linked, measured, and quoted briefly for criticism and analysis** — as done throughout doc 728 — which is a different act from redistribution.

### 3.2 The permissive sources

`NVIDIA/skills`, `NVIDIA/nurec-skills`, `NVIDIA-Medtech/medical-AI-skills`, `agentskills/agentskills`, `minio/skills`, and `google/skills` are **Apache-2.0** (some dual with CC-BY-4.0 for docs); `microsoft/skills` is **MIT**; the three `aws-samples` repositories are **MIT-0**.

**Apache-2.0 and MIT permit reuse WITH attribution and license preservation.** If we reuse any file, schema, or non-trivial code fragment from those sources, we must: retain the copyright notice and license text, state the changes made (Apache-2.0 § 4(b)), and record the source and its resolved commit SHA in `.source.json` and in our `NOTICE`/`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES`. **MIT-0 waives the attribution requirement**, but we attribute anyway — cheap, and rank 2/rank 3 of the decision hierarchy make honest attribution the default regardless of what a license permits us to omit.

**Practical rule for executing this ADR:** where an ADOPT is sourced from a permissive repository and we choose to reuse actual content (a schema shape, a workflow skeleton), the reuse is recorded like any other mirror — resolved SHA, license text carried with the bytes, notices updated. Where we merely learn from the pattern, no obligation attaches and none is claimed.

### 3.3 What we ship

Two repositories in the sample ship a third-party-licenses file that is **factually contradicted by their own tree** (one asserts "does not bundle any third-party assets" while 56 vendor SVGs sit in the repo; another vendors three real marketplace skills with `NOTICE → 404`). **At mirror scale, an untrue license file is worse than no license file.** Our 63-mirror pipeline therefore requires `redistribution.allowed` as a **hard refusal**, not a warning; a `NOTICE`/`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` with a forward obligation; and per-skill license fields whose absence **blocks** mirroring.

## 7. COMPATIBILITY CONSEQUENCES

- **Canonical frontmatter remains a strict superset of the open Agent Skills spec** and is therefore non-conformant to that spec's reference validator (~3,100 of our skills would fail it today, because it errors on unknown top-level keys). The **generated conformance projection** — dual-writing `version`, `author`, and `tags` into the spec-legal `metadata:` string map — makes every shipped artifact spec-legal **without touching `ALWAYS_REQUIRED`.**
- **`compatible-with` stays deprecated.** Free-text `compatibility` becomes a generated projection of `adapters[]`; no surface reads a hand-authored string.
- **Agents keep camelCase `disallowedTools`; skills keep kebab-case `disallowed-tools`.** The mismatch is intentional and the validator rejects either substitution.
- **The public install slug `jeremylongshore/claude-code-plugins` is frozen and now declared as such** (`compat.install_slug` in the registry, annotated as a frozen API). It is hardcoded in the CLI, the Hero snippet, and hundreds of READMEs, and GitHub's 301 to the canonical repository name is load-bearing. **Any rename is a breaking API change.** This constraint applies to every layout proposal in this ADR — which is why § 6 rejects a flat canonical root.
- **The three required CI contexts stay exactly three** (`ci-required` + `gitleaks` + `skill-conform`). Every new blocking check is a job inside `validate-plugins.yml`, listed in `ci-required.needs`. Adding a fourth required context would resurrect the stuck-PR class this repo already fixed.
- **Existing skills are not retroactively made non-compliant.** `skill-card.yaml` is required only at tier T2+, `eval-spec.yaml` only at T3+, integrity digests and negative fixtures only at T4. Tier is computed, never self-declared.
- **Mirrors are never edited by us.** Their improvement path is upstream contribution under the existing mirror-by-default model; a `curated:` freeze remains the only mechanism by which a mirror carries local hardening.

## 8. MIGRATION IMPACT

**No renames of `plugins/`, `skills/`, or the install slug — therefore zero breakage for the hundreds of published READMEs and the CLI.** The real migrations are five, all reversible:

1. **Catalog authorship** may eventually move to a per-plugin registry with `marketplace.extended.json` flipping to generated — **deferred**, with a named trigger (§ 5). Until then, the two-catalog contract is unchanged.
2. **`skill-card.yaml` is introduced tier-gated**, so no existing skill becomes non-compliant on the day the schema lands.
3. **Free-text `compatibility` becomes generated**, which withdraws 1,454 untested portability claims. This is visible and is the intended outcome: an untested claim is withdrawn, not a capability removed.
4. **The verification badge goes dark** until an evidence class supports it — 3 ledger rows demote to E0 because their primary artifacts were never retained.
5. **~313 mirrors stop carrying a marketplace grade** they did not earn; they become QUARANTINE or CERTIFY-UPSTREAM with the grade disclosed as upstream's.

**Corpus remediation runs on a bounded rolling cadence and does not begin until the compliance ratchet is live** — otherwise remediation and new intake are indistinguishable — **and an independent review must close clean before the first certified cohort is published.**

**Cost:** a full behavioral sweep of the corpus prices at roughly **$6.3k** (3,179 × ~$2). The first certified cohort is capped at 10–15 skills (~$100–300) and is approved per cohort, never per run.

## 9. WHY THIS DESIGN BEST SERVES INTENT SOLUTIONS

Four reasons, in hierarchy order.

**It closes the legal and identity exposure first, and the sequencing is not negotiable.** 58 mirror packages are publishable today through an ungated path, one of them conveying copyleft content with no license text. Rank 2 outranks every quality consideration in this document, so the first action is quarantine — a single additive, revertible commit — not a grade improvement. No other reference architecture in the sample even models this class of risk; two of them ship license files that are factually false about their own trees.

**It refuses to create new authorities, which is the actual failure mode this repo has.** Mirror mis-attribution, one-fact-two-claimants twice, ~200 silently gitignored docs — none of those was a missing capability. Every element of this design either removes a claimant (generated adapters, generated compatibility, one corpus resolver, one measurement harness) or names the single writer of a fact class (kernel owns the schema; the Lab owns eval verdicts; Intent OS owns host facts; beads own task state). The one genuinely new artifact — the skill card — exists specifically so the frontmatter stops accreting claimants.

**It makes the honest answer cheap and the dishonest answer expensive.** Evidence classes with retention as a validity condition mean an unbacked badge goes dark automatically rather than requiring someone to notice. A `recall_note` on every score means an **A** cannot silently read as "this works." A ratchet keyed on `(path, rule, field)` means debt can only shrink, and only a bot can write the baseline. A certification that cannot be self-approved and that expires means a claim cannot quietly become historical. Every one of these makes truthfulness the default path rather than a discipline someone has to maintain.

**It is the only design in the sample that can hold a real bar over a real corpus.** Uniform anatomy at 3,179 skills has two outcomes and both are bad. Tier-gating is the mechanism that lets us publish "12 certified, 3,668 pending" — which is a stronger and more defensible public claim than "1,596 A-grade" backed by a rubric that 963 gate-failing artifacts already contradict. And we can back it: Dolt is the append-only public record that **none** of the twelve benchmarked repositories has, and it turns our grade history into exactly the transparency log that NVIDIA's signing story is missing.

**Recommended direction: adopt this architecture; execute the blueprint's epics in the order its critical path specifies; and do not begin corpus migration until the compliance ratchet is live and an independent review closes clean.**

## 10. CONSEQUENCES

**What becomes true.** One canonical contract with a machine-checkable portability claim. Adapters that cannot drift because nothing hand-writes them. Mirrors that cannot be republished under our identity. Evidence that carries its class and its artifact hash. A public certified set with its backlog printed beside it. A documentation estate with exactly one authoritative owner per fact class.

**What becomes harder.** Adding a plugin now requires a license and a resolved upstream SHA. Claiming multi-harness support now requires an adapter artifact. Publishing a badge now requires a retained, hash-matched artifact and an independent signer. A contributor cannot self-waive a severity. None of these is accidental friction; each is a rank-1-to-rank-4 control that was previously absent.

**What we now owe.** A kernel issue proposing the `skill-contract`, `capability`, and `eval-spec` schemas so this repo never becomes its own schema authority (blueprint Epic 3). A `NOTICE`/`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` that is true about our own tree. An AI-assistance disclosure line in `CONTRIBUTING.md`. And the eight escalations in blueprint § 18, which this ADR does not resolve.

**Rollback.** Every element is additive and revertible except three, which are called out wherever they appear: moving `NPM_TOKEN` into a protected Environment (owner-executed, not `git revert`-able), rotating a credential (irreversible by definition), and any Rekor entry (immutable by design — revocation is a superseding signed event, never a deletion).

**Enforced by.** Nothing in this document is self-enforcing. Each adopted pattern names its gate in `000-docs/727-AT-ARCH-master-modernization-blueprint.md`; anything that lands without a gate is, by our own rank-5 rule, prose — and must be labelled as such.
