## Deviations from § 6A.2, disclosed

1. **The Killer-Skill spotlight block is retained.** The § 6A.2 IA does not list it; it is
   editorial (owner-picked weekly), generated by its own bounded writer, and small. Removing it
   would break the `/promote-spotlight` tooling and an owner-valued surface without a § 6A rule
   requiring it.
2. **Header badges keep integer values.** They are regenerated by the single writer
   (`applyStats`) from the same cohorts the SCALE block labels; a badge cannot carry a command,
   so the SCALE block is the labeled authority and the badges are its projection.
3. **NPM-STATS block retained** with its own daily writer — it predates this slice, is
   sentinel-bounded, and removing it would break `fetch-npm-stats.mjs`'s daily refresh contract.
