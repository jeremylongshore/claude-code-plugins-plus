# 728 — Reference Architecture Benchmark: 12 Primary-Source Repositories

**Status:** REFERENCE — the evidence base behind `000-docs/727-AT-ARCH-master-modernization-blueprint.md` and `000-docs/729-AT-ADEC-reference-architecture-synthesis.md`.
**Doc class:** `canonical` (data record; regenerate only by re-reviewing at new SHAs)
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Method:** primary-source review of each repository at a pinned immutable commit. Every material claim below is a file-level observation at the SHA in § 1. `ABSENT` means a researcher confirmed nonexistence. `UNVERIFIED` means it could not be checked and is carried forward as a gap, never smoothed over.

**Scale framing, stated once up front.** The entire public benchmark set contains roughly **741 `SKILL.md` across 12 repositories.** The Intent Solutions corpus alone is **3,179 plugin `SKILL.md`**. The largest repository in the sample — `NVIDIA/skills` at 336 — is **10.6% of our size**, and it is visibly failing at that size (24% of its catalog frozen in signature drift; its integrity sweep red for 10 consecutive days). **No repository in this sample has solved a problem at our scale.** Every ADOPT in § 4 is the adoption of a _shape_, never of a proven-at-scale implementation. Any claim of the form "X does it this way, so it works" is unsupported by this evidence base.

### 3b. The proposed authority flow — validated, with three corrections

> Proposed: canonical portable skill → validated harness adapters → generated plugin/package distributions → marketplace + website projections.

**The spine is correct and is corroborated by the strongest repository in the sample** (`NVIDIA/skills`: "The `skills/` directory is the single source of truth — every SKILL.md exists exactly once there. The plugin tree is reconstructed from these YAML files on every build"). Three corrections are required by the evidence:

**Correction 1 — "validated harness adapters" must read "GENERATED harness adapters, drift-gated."** _Validated_ implies hand-authored-then-checked. Every hand-authored adapter layer in the sample has drifted: AWS-WA's fork lost the entire coverage architecture (16× "307" → 0) **while the installer ships both surfaces**, so the repo's headline F1 = 0.960 attaches to a path most users never take; google maintains two marketplace dialects by hand and they already carry different field sets. The only healthy adapter layers are generated (NVIDIA) or symlinked-and-`--check`-gated (microsoft). _(rank 4, rank 3 — a stale adapter makes a public performance claim false.)_

**Correction 2 — insert a class the flow omits: MIRRORS.** Mirrors are neither canonical nor generated; they are _ingested_. IS has 63 of them and has already been burned once — a mirror-detection bug republished 6 external plugins under Intent Solutions' name, a rank-2/rank-3 failure. The flow must read:

```
canonical portable skill  ─┐
                           ├─► GENERATED harness adapters ─► GENERATED plugin/package
ingested mirror            │   distributions ─► marketplace + website projections
(quarantined,              ▲
 provenance-bound,         │
 never promotable) ────────┘
```

A mirror may be _distributed_ but may never enter the canonical lane, may never be authored against, and must carry upstream identity through every downstream projection.

**Correction 3 — name the sidecar explicitly in the flow.** "Canonical portable skill" must mean _frontmatter (agent-facing, tiny, uniform) **plus** a closed-schema machine manifest_. Without that split, the canonical artifact accretes operational metadata until it looks like NVIDIA's 30-key free-for-all, and the description-injection budget — the one thing that actually decides whether a skill fires — gets crowded out.

**The rest of the flow is validated as written.** "No adapter, plugin package, marketplace file, mirror, or website representation may silently become canonical authority" is precisely the `agentskills/agentskills` `AGENTS.md` authority statement — the best governance artifact in all 12 repos.

## 5. VENDOR-CARGO-CULT WARNINGS

Specific places where copying a big-name repository would **actively harm** this platform.

**W1 — "Anthropic's own skills repo is the reference implementation."** It is not, and citing it as authority is the 2026-04-28 realign-to-the-floor debacle in a new costume. At the reviewed SHA `anthropics/skills` has: **no `.github/` and zero CI**; a hand-edited marketplace manifest (a stray comma proves it); **2,282,167 redundant bytes across 53 duplicate blob groups at only 17 skills**; a 74,327-byte SKILL.md violating its own <500-line guidance; **two skills with no license at all in a repo whose license field is `null`**; and 0 tags / 0 releases / no `version` anywhere. **Every one of its 17 skills would fail the IS marketplace tier.** 169k stars measure adoption of the _format_, not quality of the _repo_. Use the format; do not import the practice.

**W2 — "Ship signatures like NVIDIA."** NVIDIA's signing is Sigstore _format_ over a private PKI with **zero transparency-log entries in 336 bundles** — no public log, no published revocation or key-compromise story. Its live state: **80 skills frozen in drift, 2 mismatched, 1 unsigned; 7 skills shipping content that fails public verification right now; the daily integrity sweep red 10 consecutive runs.** And the trust artifact **fails the trust scanner** — the signature file itself trips the security scanner, flipping two skills to `DO_NOT_INSTALL`. Copying this at 3,179 skills buys a permanent, human-gated stale-content backlog. **Adopt content-addressed digests (F8); reject the PKI.**

**W3 — "Backfill missing metadata with an LLM, like NVIDIA's taxonomy generator."** NVIDIA's PR gate "fails on drift," so **a new skill lacking taxonomy fields cannot merge without an AI-enriched regeneration run.** On an already-AI-authored corpus that is (a) a merge deadlock waiting to happen and (b) a **rank 3** problem — machine-invented taxonomy presented downstream as authored metadata. If IS ever auto-fills catalog fields, the artifact must record that they were generated, and the generated value must never become the provenance claim.

**W4 — "Adopt the medtech per-skill trust harness."** It is the most rigorous contract in the sample and it is **12 skills**, all hand-authored, with **no generators and no stable harness API**, and its real gates run **locally on a maintainer's workstation, deliberately not in CI**. Manifest + output schema + fixtures + tests + validators + evals + benchmark + card + signature + a paired verifier, times 3,179, is roughly 30,000 hand-authored artifacts. You would get fake compliance, not quality. **Take the two-layer identity split and the side-effect schema; leave the harness.**

**W5 — "Vendor third-party plugins as tag-pinned git submodules, like Google."** Elegant, and the only cryptographic integrity binding anywhere in Panel C — and still wrong here. Submodules make mirrored content **invisible to `scan-synced-content`** (nothing checked out to scan), **unbuildable by `build-cowork-zips`**, and require a submodule init for every consumer and CI job. Google also ships **16 submodules with no NOTICE file**, so the mechanism did not even deliver the attribution hygiene it is praised for. **Get the same guarantee by adding a resolved commit SHA to `.source.json` (B1).**

**W6 — "Microsoft built a real gate, so follow Microsoft's CI."** Microsoft is the only Panel-C repo with a genuine eval harness **and it cannot fail a build**: the harness is wrapped in `set +e` and forced to `exit 0`. Its validator reads **one directory level**, so 11 of 198 skills (5.6%) are structurally invisible — **and that is exactly where the three frontmatter-less `SKILL.md` files are sitting at HEAD.** Symlink drift is checked for 2 of 10 plugins. At n=198 this already produces four mutually contradictory skill counts in one repo. Do not import the posture, and specifically do not import the sibling rationalization — "plumbing drift is eventually consistent, the cron self-heals" — in a comment that is itself wrong about the cron cadence.

**W7 — "Follow the open standard's six-field frontmatter."** Directionally right for interop, catastrophic if read as "so drop the IS 8." The reference validator makes `version`, `author`, and `tags` **errors**; the spec itself is **unversioned with 0 tags and no changelog**; its `metadata` is **string→string only** (no lists, no nesting); and **no workflow runs its own tests** — the reference validator the entire ecosystem is told to trust is unexercised in CI and self-labels "demonstration purposes only." **The correct move is dual-write (A3), not surrender.**

**W8 — "Import AWS's grading model."** AWS-EVAL's own published audit gives three widely-used document skills **0/100 (F)** — from 34 occurrences of **XML namespace URLs** read as an exfiltration surface — and another skill 38/F from five `npm install` lines. A flat −10 per finding with no cardinality damping means one repeated benign pattern zeroes any skill. **The IS 7,687-error marketplace baseline is the same shape**: an ungraded error mass nobody can act on. Any scoring change must damp by cardinality and cluster by root cause first.

**W9 — "Hand-write per-harness adapters like AWS-WA."** AWS-WA's canonical skill is 638 lines with 16 mentions of "307", 6 subagent dispatches and 3 ledger references; its hand-forked command file is 270 lines with **zero of any of them** — and the installer **installs both**, so the slash-command path runs the measured-to-fail architecture while the README's headline "F1 = 0.960" attaches only to the other path. **The manual install instructions ship the stale path exclusively.** That is a false public performance claim produced purely by an unguarded adapter layer. At 14 hosts × 3,200 skills the hand-fork model is ~44,800 files no CI checks.

**W10 — "Nobody has deprecation, so we don't need it."** Twelve of twelve is a shared blind spot, not a verdict. NVIDIA deletes silently and lets a third-party CLI diff for users; google renames with no alias or tombstone; microsoft's compat symlink farm has no sunset date; openai deprecated a repo without archiving it and then shipped a feature into it. **IS carries a constraint none of them do** — a legacy install slug hardcoded in hundreds of READMEs — so silent deletion is a breaking API change here in a way it is not for them.

**W11 — "Publish the catalog in the README."** NVIDIA: 50,315 bytes at 336 skills, with 63 skills on one table row (≈500 KB at our size). Microsoft: 762 hand-maintained lines producing four contradictory counts. Google: the README lists ~105 against 109 on disk, under a **third** taxonomy that matches neither its metadata vocabulary nor its catalog. IS's AUTO-TOC is generated and therefore already better than all three — **the risk is scope creep into per-skill rows, not the mechanism.**

**W12 — "Use symlinks for compatibility paths."** Microsoft's 60 symlinks break on any checkout without symlink support, and only 2 of 10 plugins are drift-checked. NVIDIA documents the operational reason it matters: **"Codex drops symlinks during install"** — hard-won knowledge worth having, and a decisive argument against a symlink farm.

**W13 — "Mirror everything, mirrors are cheap."** They are the most expensive category of mistake in this sample: AWS-EVAL vendors three real third-party marketplace skills verbatim with **NOTICE → 404 and THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES → 404**; AWS-OPS ships a third-party-licenses file asserting "does not bundle any third-party assets" while 56 vendor icon SVGs sit in the tree; microsoft push-mirrors a **925-file plugin (47% of the repo) with zero provenance metadata** and no ability to re-pull, verify, or diff its own mirror; openai shipped another vendor's contributor eval assets verbatim into its catalog. **IS has already lived the consequence once** (6 external plugins republished under IS's name). Mirrors are a **rank 2 and rank 3** surface before they are anything else.
