# Scorecard Marketing -- Implementation Guide

## Overview

Apply Glenn Carlson's scorecard marketing methodology to generate qualified leads using interactive assessments that deliver personalized insights.

## Why Scorecards Work

- Deliver immediate personalized value (not generic lead magnets)
- Self-qualify leads by revealing their situation
- Create natural follow-up context ("your score was X, here's what that means")

## Scorecard Architecture

### Step 1: Define the Transformation

```markdown
Before state (pain): "Prospects are unsure about [their problem area]"
After state (gain): "They have clarity on exactly where to focus"

Scorecard title: "[Audience] [Outcome] Assessment"
Example: "Startup Marketing Readiness Score"
```

### Step 2: Choose 3-5 Pillar Categories

```markdown
Categories (each scored 0-10):

1. [Pillar] -- "How well are you [doing X]?"
2. [Pillar] -- "How effectively are you [doing Y]?"
3. [Pillar] -- "To what extent is your [Z] in place?"
```

### Step 3: Write 3-5 Questions Per Category

```markdown
Question format: "On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate your [specific aspect]?"

Avoid: Vague or too complex questions
Use: Observable, concrete behaviors

Example:
"We consistently post on LinkedIn at least 3x per week."
1 (Not at all) -- 5 (Completely describes us)
```

### Step 4: Define Score Segments

```markdown
Score bands (out of 100):

0-40: [Label] -- Brief description of their situation + urgent call to action
41-70: [Label] -- Describe their state + next steps
71-100: [Label] -- Congratulate + premium CTA
```

### Step 5: Follow-Up Email Sequence

```markdown
Email 1 (immediate): Score + interpretation + one key insight
Email 2 (day 3): Deep dive on their weakest category
Email 3 (day 7): Case study of someone who improved that area
Email 4 (day 14): Direct offer linked to their specific score
```
