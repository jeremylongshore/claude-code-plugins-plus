# Contagious -- STEPPS Framework Implementation Guide

## Overview

Apply Jonah Berger's STEPPS framework (Social Currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public, Practical Value, Stories) to engineer word-of-mouth virality for products, campaigns, and content.

## The STEPPS Framework

### 1. Social Currency

**Principle:** People share things that make them look good.

**Application checklist:**
- [ ] Does using/sharing this make the user look smart, cool, or in-the-know?
- [ ] Is there an inner-circle effect (exclusive access, limited availability)?
- [ ] Does it have game mechanics (streaks, levels, visible progress)?

**Example prompt to assess:**
> "Rate this feature on Social Currency (1-10): Does sharing it signal status or insider knowledge?"

### 2. Triggers

**Principle:** Top of mind = tip of tongue. Associate your product with frequent environmental cues.

**Application:**
- Map usage context to daily triggers (morning coffee, commute, end-of-workday)
- Identify "habitat" -- when and where do users naturally think about the problem?
- Design notifications/nudges aligned to those moments

**Trigger audit template:**
```
Product: [name]
Natural usage moment: [e.g., "when finishing a meeting"]
Environmental trigger: [e.g., "calendar event ends"]
Trigger-to-action link: [e.g., "meeting ended -> open summary tool"]
```

### 3. Emotion

**Principle:** High-arousal emotions (awe, anxiety, excitement, humor) drive sharing. Low-arousal (sadness, contentment) do not.

**Emotion mapping:**
| Emotion | Arousal | Valence | Share Rate |
|---------|---------|---------|-----------|
| Awe | High | Positive | Very High |
| Anxiety | High | Negative | High |
| Amusement | High | Positive | High |
| Sadness | Low | Negative | Low |
| Contentment | Low | Positive | Low |

### 4. Public (Observability)

**Principle:** Designed to show, built to grow. Visible usage creates social proof.

**Tactics:**
- Add social indicators ("X people are using this right now")
- Enable public sharing with branded output (PDF exports, share links with logo)
- Create behavioral residue (stickers, "Made with X" watermarks)

### 5. Practical Value

**Principle:** Useful information gets passed along. News you can use.

**Application:**
- Identify the one actionable insight your product delivers
- Package it in a shareable format (infographic, checklist, template)
- Frame in terms of savings: time, money, risk reduction

### 6. Stories

**Principle:** Information travels under the guise of idle chatter. Embed your message in a narrative.

**Story structure:**
```
Hook: [Relatable struggle]
Conflict: [What stood in the way]
Resolution: [How your product/idea solved it]
Moral: [The message you want to spread]
```
