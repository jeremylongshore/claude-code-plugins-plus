# Predictable Revenue -- Outbound Sales Implementation Guide

## Overview

Apply Aaron Ross's Predictable Revenue model (Cold Calling 2.0, seed/net/spear strategies, and specialization) to build a reliable outbound pipeline.

## Core Principles

1. **No cold calling** -- use targeted cold emails to generate warm conversations
2. **Specialization** -- separate prospecting, closing, and account management roles
3. **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)** -- only prospect accounts that can actually buy

## Cold Email System (Cold Calling 2.0)

### Step 1: Research and List Building

```markdown
Sources: LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo.io, ZoomInfo, G2 Buyer Intent

List building criteria:
- Match ICP firmographics
- Decision maker title: [VP/Director/Head of ___]
- Company recently: hired for [role] / raised funding / launched new product
```

### Step 2: The "Referral Email"

Send to a senior executive. Ask for a referral to the right person.

```
Subject: [Name], quick question

Hi [Name],

I'm reaching out about [specific challenge your ICP faces].
We work with [2-3 company names they'd recognize] to help them 
[specific result in 1 sentence].

Who on your team would be the right person to talk to about this?

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

### Step 3: Follow-up Sequence

```markdown
Day 1: Initial email (above)
Day 3: Follow-up: "Did you see my note?"
Day 7: Value email (share a resource, no ask)
Day 14: Break-up email: "Should I close your file?"
```

## Specialization Model

```markdown
## Sales Team Roles

Market Response Reps (MRRs): Handle inbound leads only
Sales Development Reps (SDRs): Outbound prospecting only, no closing
Account Executives (AEs): Close and onboard only
Customer Success Managers (CSMs): Retain and expand only

Never mix prospecting with closing: kills performance in both.
```
