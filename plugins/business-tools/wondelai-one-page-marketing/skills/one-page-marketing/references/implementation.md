# One Page Marketing Plan -- Implementation Guide

## Overview

Apply Allan Dib's one-page marketing plan framework to define your before, during, and after customer journey in a single structured document.

## The One-Page Marketing Plan (9 Squares)

```
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
|   BEFORE         |   DURING         |   AFTER          |
| (Prospects)      | (Leads)          | (Customers)      |
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
| 1. Target Market | 4. Lead Capture  | 7. Delivering    |
|                  |    System        |    World-Class   |
|                  |                  |    Experience    |
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
| 2. Message to    | 5. Lead Nurture  | 8. Lifetime      |
|    Market        |    System        |    Value         |
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
| 3. Media to      | 6. Sales         | 9. Referrals     |
|    Reach Them    |    Conversion    |                  |
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
```

## Square-by-Square Completion Guide

### Square 1: Target Market

```markdown
Who is your single best customer persona?

Name: [persona name]
Psychographics: [what they care about, fear, desire]
Pain point you solve: [specific, not generic]
Where they hang out: [media, communities, events]
```

### Square 2: Message to Market

```markdown
Your USP (Unique Selling Proposition):

Complete this: "We help [persona] achieve [dream outcome] 
in [time frame] even if [common objection], guaranteed."

Your irresistible offer: [specific offer with risk reversal]
```

### Square 3: Media Channels

```markdown
Rank your top 3 channels (reach x cost-effectiveness):

1. ___  (budget: $___/mo, expected leads: ___)
2. ___  (budget: $___/mo, expected leads: ___)
3. ___  (budget: $___/mo, expected leads: ___)

Goal: One channel mastered before adding the next.
```

### Square 4: Lead Capture System

```markdown
Lead magnet: [free thing they want that qualifies them]
Capture mechanism: [landing page, form, phone]
CRM/database: [where you store leads]
```

### Square 5: Lead Nurture System

```markdown
Nurture sequence:
Week 1: [educational content]
Week 2: [case study / social proof]
Week 3: [direct offer]
Week 4+: [ongoing value content]

Frequency: ___/week  Channel: email / SMS / social
```

### Squares 6-9: Conversion, Experience, LTV, Referrals

```markdown
Conversion: What is your sales process? [script / meeting flow]
Experience: What is your onboarding / delivery system?
LTV: How do you upsell / retain customers?
Referrals: What is your referral trigger? (automated ask at [moment])
```

## One-Page Plan Review Cadence

- Monthly: Review lead/conversion metrics for each square
- Quarterly: Identify the weakest square and focus 80% of effort there
- Annually: Full plan refresh
