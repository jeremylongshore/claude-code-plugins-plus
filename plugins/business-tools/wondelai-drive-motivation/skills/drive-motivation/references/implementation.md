# Drive -- Motivation Science Implementation Guide

## Overview

Apply Daniel Pink's Drive framework (Autonomy, Mastery, Purpose) to design motivating work environments, performance systems, and team structures.

## The Three Elements of Intrinsic Motivation

### 1. Autonomy

People need control over four dimensions of work:

| Dimension | Low Autonomy Example | High Autonomy Example |
|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Task | Assigned tickets only | 20% time / self-selected projects |
| Time | Fixed hours, no flexibility | Results-only work environment (ROWE) |
| Technique | Prescribed methodology | Choose your own tools/approach |
| Team | Assigned team, no say | Self-organizing teams, internal hiring |

**Autonomy audit questions:**
```markdown
1. Can engineers choose which problems to work on (at least sometimes)?
2. Can they choose HOW to solve problems (tools, approach)?
3. Can they set their own schedule at least partially?
4. Can they choose who to work with on optional projects?
```

### 2. Mastery

Mastery requires flow state: challenge slightly exceeds current skill level.

```markdown
## Mastery Conditions Checklist

- [ ] Are goals clear and unambiguous?
- [ ] Is feedback immediate (not waiting for annual review)?
- [ ] Is the work challenging but not overwhelming?
- [ ] Is there a progression path (junior -> senior -> staff)?
- [ ] Are people learning new skills this quarter?
```

**Mastery-blocking patterns to eliminate:**
- Pure maintenance work with no learning opportunity
- Constant context-switching (prevents flow)
- Feedback only at annual reviews
- Ceiling on growth (no path forward)

### 3. Purpose

Connecting work to something larger than profit maximizes engagement.

```markdown
## Purpose Statement Framework

**For individuals:** "My work matters because it [direct impact on real people]."
**For teams:** "We exist to [mission] so that [beneficiary]."
**For companies:** Beyond maximizing shareholder value -- what is the animating goal?
```

## Applying Drive to Performance Management

### Replace carrot-and-stick with:

```markdown
## Performance System Redesign

Old (carrot/stick):
- Sales commission per deal
- Bonus for hitting quarterly target
- Punishment for missing KPIs

New (autonomy/mastery/purpose):
- Profit sharing (whole company wins together)
- Learning budget (individual mastery investment)
- 20% time (autonomy to explore)
- Team mission statement (purpose visibility)
- Regular 1:1 feedback (mastery tracking)
```

## Motivation Diagnostic

```markdown
## Team Motivation Survey (monthly)

Rate 1-5:
1. I have enough control over how I do my work. [Autonomy]
2. I am getting better at my craft this month. [Mastery]
3. My work connects to something that matters. [Purpose]
4. I feel energized by my work most days.
5. I know what good looks like for my role.

Action threshold: Any item below 3.5 average = investigate immediately.
```
